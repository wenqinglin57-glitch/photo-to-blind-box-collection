# Intake and photo mapping

## Ask the project mode first

Before requesting the full photo set, ask one short question: is this a **two-person collection** or a **single-person collection**?

- **Two-person collection:** request three different life photos, each showing both people in a distinct scene. Each photo produces two separate single-person figures, for six figures total.
- **Single-person collection:** request six different life photos of the same person, one scene per figure.
- A source photo may contain one or two people, but if the submitted set does not match the selected mode, ask the user to confirm an explicit mapping that still produces exactly six single-person figures.

Do not begin by generically asking for six photos. Establish the mode first so the requested number and mapping are correct.

## Required intake fields

Collect the following before generating:

1. The required three or six source photos.
2. The name or short label for every person and an unambiguous left/right or clothing-based identification in each two-person photo.
3. The six-item numbering map. Default for a two-person project: scene 1 becomes 01 and 02, scene 2 becomes 03 and 04, and scene 3 becomes 05 and 06.
4. Details that must be retained or removed for each scene: hairstyle, glasses, hats, outfit colors, shoes, props, hand pose, and any unwanted object.
5. Stable appearance rules across scenes, such as relative height, face shape, eye size, eyewear, hairstyle, and recurring accessories.
6. An optional preferred blind-box style reference. If none is supplied, use the default style in `figure-workflow.md`.

If a crucial face, shoe, accessory, or logo detail is unreadable, request one close-up supplemental photo. A supplemental detail photo does not count as one of the three or six scene photos.

## Internal mapping record

Maintain a table like this throughout the project:

| Figure | Source scene | Person | Outfit and props | Required pose | Must remove |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Scene 1 | Person A | ... | ... | ... |

Use this map for rendering, silhouettes, packaging, and cards. Never silently swap the numbering.

## Privacy

Treat all portraits and life photos as private source material. Use them only for the requested figures, packaging, and cards. Do not add inferred personal information.
