# Six-figure workflow

## Build an identity bible

Before rendering, record a compact identity bible for each recurring person:

- overall face shape and cheek fullness;
- eye shape, size, spacing, and vertical placement;
- one consistent nose design shared across that person's six appearances;
- mouth shape and usual expression;
- hair color, part, length, and tied or loose state;
- glasses shape, frame thickness, and color;
- relative height and body proportion;
- stable distinguishing details the user explicitly selected.

Use the identity bible to keep the same person recognizable across outfits and scenes. If two people appear, keep them distinct rather than averaging their faces.

## Default figure style

Unless the user supplies another approved reference, use:

- cute high-end 3D chibi collectible figure;
- compact proportions without an excessively babyish head-to-body ratio;
- smooth high-gloss PVC/plastic material with controlled highlights;
- clean studio background and soft, even lighting;
- simple sculptable hands and readable props;
- full-body standing composition with both shoes visible;
- no base and no heavy facial shadow.

## Convert each scene into one or two figures

- Preserve identity, scene-specific outfit, hairstyle, footwear, pose, and meaningful prop unless the user asks for a change.
- Simplify tiny logos and fabric micro-detail into manufacturable shapes without changing the main colors or silhouette.
- For a two-person source, separate shared contact poses and props carefully. Each result must stand alone as a plausible single-person figure.
- Do not turn scenery into a pedestal or base.
- Keep hand anatomy clean and verify that props are held by the intended hand.

## Production order

1. Render each figure as an individual full-body studio image.
2. Check identity, outfit, pose, hands, footwear, and figure completeness against the mapping record.
3. Apply one targeted correction pass for obvious discrepancies.
4. Create a clean transparent-background cutout from the approved render.
5. Assemble a numbered 01–06 contact sheet at equal visual scale.
6. Derive each silhouette from the approved full-body cutout, including meaningful hat, hair, garment, pose, and prop contours.

## Figure approval gate

Before packaging, show the numbered contact sheet and confirm:

- exactly six single-person figures are present;
- every figure matches its source scene and assigned person;
- recurring faces follow the identity bible;
- different people remain visually distinct;
- full heads, hands, garments, and shoes are visible;
- there are no extra limbs, accidental props, scenery, or bases;
- the six silhouettes are recognizable as the approved six designs.

Stop and obtain approval before the packaging phase.
