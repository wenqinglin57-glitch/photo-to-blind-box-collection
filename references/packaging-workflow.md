# Packaging workflow

## Fixed production specification

- Finished box: 100 mm wide × 80 mm deep × 140 mm high.
- Bleed: 3 mm.
- Glue flap: 15 mm.
- Print resolution: 300 DPI.
- Keep all critical text and faces inside a safe area appropriate to the printer and dieline.

## Packaging intake

Ask for these project-specific values before design:

1. Product name. This replaces every inherited `L²` or previous title.
2. Designer name.
3. Applicable age. Ask every project; do not silently reuse an earlier age.
4. One front-scene reference photo and any objects that must be retained or removed.
5. Which approved figure or figures appear on the front. Default: both people for a two-person project, the one person for a single-person project.
6. Calendar year, month, and the date to circle with a heart.
7. Desired calendar-character state. Suggest an anniversary pose with both characters raising their arms to form one heart, or a birthday pose holding a cake, but accept any clear requested state.
8. The preferred coordinated solid-color mood for all non-front panels. If omitted, derive it from the front scene.

Always ask for the calendar year even if only the month name is printed, because weekday placement must be correct.

## Front panel

- Transform the supplied scene into a watercolor or ink-wash scenic illustration while preserving its recognizable spatial relationship and requested objects.
- Remove only the objects the user explicitly asks to remove.
- Composite the approved 3D figure cutouts into the scene; do not redraw their faces, clothes, or body proportions in the background style.
- Default to both people for a two-person collection and one person for a single-person collection.
- Keep every head, hand, prop, and shoe inside the safe area.
- Display the user-provided product name in place of all inherited `L²` marks.

Show the finished front panel and obtain approval before building the full flat plan.

## Back panel

Set these items as separate lines:

- `产品名称：[产品名称]`
- `设计师：[设计师姓名]`
- `主要材质：Love`
- `适用年龄：[本项目询问的年龄]`

Do not include an execution standard, production address, or service line.

Include the warning text exactly:

`全球仅此一份请妥善保管，请勿丢弃。`

The warning character uses the same primary character form and outfit as the front, with the established angry expression and warning gesture. In a two-person project, use the left or first front character unless the user chooses the other person.

## Calendar panel

- Build a real calendar from the confirmed year and month.
- Use the requested month title and circle the confirmed date with a heart.
- Ask for the character pose. Offer an overhead shared-heart pose for an anniversary or a cake-holding pose for a birthday.
- Use the approved front character forms unless the user selects different figures.
- Scale the characters so heads and raised hands remain fully inside the panel.

## Collection panel

- Default heading: `Who will you meet?`
- In a two-person front, use the other front person's approved big-head character; in a single-person front, reuse the same person.
- Keep the established puzzled expression and pose holding one artistic question mark.
- Show six framed silhouettes derived from the six approved figures. Each frame has exactly one question mark on the silhouette and `1/6` at the lower right.

## Other panels, flaps, and surrounding area

- Only the front panel contains the transformed scene.
- Back, calendar, collection, top, bottom, and all flaps use coordinated solid colors derived from the front artwork. Do not repeat chairs, balloons, buildings, flowers, or other scene objects on these surfaces unless the user explicitly requests them.
- Use a narrow bleed extension of the assigned solid color beyond each cut edge.
- The area outside the packaging dieline is clean white.
- Replace all inherited product marks with the current product name.

## Packaging quality gate

Verify:

- dieline size, bleed, glue flap, cut lines, and fold lines;
- all dates against the confirmed year and month;
- exact product name, designer, material, and age;
- no execution-standard line;
- front figures match the approved renders and are not cropped;
- non-front panels are solid color as specified;
- six silhouettes match the six figures and each says `1/6`;
- all space outside the dieline is white.
